Costs

Three separate layers, never double-counted. (1) Trading charges (STT, exchange, SEBI, stamp duty, GST, DP) are paid from virtual cash and appear only in the trading account. (2) Running costs (data API, hosting, AI, data) are paid outside the virtual account and reduce economic performance, accrued per calendar day. (3) Setup costs (one-off) reduce all-in project performance only.
